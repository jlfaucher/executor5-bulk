USE STRICT ARG breaking change

I don't think we need a big discusion of this,
omitting argument names is an edge case,
and quite irrelevant to most people.
However, that doesn't mean it can't be improved.

Review the differences in the output of the test cases between ooRexx 4.2 and ooRexx 5.3.
rexx use_strict_arg_test.rex > use_strict_arg_test-out-42.txt 2>&1
rexx use_strict_arg_test.rex > use_strict_arg_test-out-53.txt 2>&1

no error in ooRexx 4.2, versus errors in ooRexx 5.3
